Every algorithmic technique implemented in the SOP solver is cataloged below in exactly one of three tables (**Table 1: Preprocessing**, **Table 2: Computation**, **Table 3: Postprocessing**). Techniques that support multiple phases are placed in the table where they perform their primary computational work, with cross-phase notes where applicable.
